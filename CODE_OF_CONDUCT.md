# Community conduct

Treat players and contributors respectfully. Harassment, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, credential sharing and attempts to compromise real systems are unacceptable. Keep feedback specific, constructive and relevant to the project. Respect camera privacy and do not record others without consent.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation. Report concerns privately to the repository owner through an available private channel; do not amplify private information in public issues. These expectations apply to project discussions, contributions and community play sessions.
